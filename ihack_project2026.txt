Interaction 1: The recipe box
When you click the recipe box:
* The lid opens.
* Recipe cards slide into view.
* Categories appear as physical dividers.
* You can browse by desserts, breads, main dishes, and more.


Interaction 2: Pulling out a recipe
When you select a card:
* It physically slides out.
* It enlarges toward the viewer.
* The handwriting and paper texture become visible.
* You can flip it to see instructions.


Interaction 3: Cooking mode
This is where your original functionality comes in.
* Ingredients can be checked off.
* Instructions advance one at a time.
* Timers can be started.
* Serving quantities adjust.
* The recipe stays visually consistent with the physical card.


Interaction 4: AI recipe transformation
This could be your biggest technological demonstration.
* A user clicks a small notebook or recipe import area.
* They paste a modern recipe URL.
* AI extracts the recipe and creates a new handwritten-style recipe card that gets placed inside the physical recipe box.
* This combines the nostalgic experience with a genuinely useful modern feature.
